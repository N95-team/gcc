WELCOME TO WTC FORMAT

The World Test Championship (WTC) is the premier league for Test cricket.

It was introduced to bring context and competition to the longest format.

Teams play a set number of matches across a cycle of two years.

Points are awarded for wins, draws, and ties in each series.

The top two teams at the end qualify for the WTC Final.

The final decides the champion of Test cricket for that cycle.

It ensures Test cricket remains exciting, competitive, and global.
